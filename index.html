Make room for rest.

Nightguard limits computer use overnight and uses your Oura Readiness score to set a daily allowance for Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps.

A clear daily rhythm

From 10pm to 9am Pacific, messaging and Apple Mail remain available. A partner-held hardware key can authorize a 25-minute override.

Time earned from Readiness

A score above 80 gives six hours shared across Claude and ChatGPT. A valid score of 80 or below gives three hours. Missing or invalid data gives two hours. Overnight restrictions still apply.
