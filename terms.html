Terms of use

Nightguard is experimental software intended for voluntary, personal management of computer use. This website describes the project and its integration.

Use and authorization

Use Nightguard only on computers and accounts you own or have permission to manage. The computer owner chooses restrictions, connects their Oura account, and designates any partner authorized to approve overrides.

Practical limits

Restrictions can interrupt access to applications. Keep a recovery method available and verify the setup before activating it. An administrator can remove the software. The current AI allowance covers desktop apps; browser coverage is not implemented.

Oura scores

The Oura integration is optional. Missing or invalid current-day scores receive the configured fallback allowance. Scores are used for a personal time rule; Nightguard does not provide medical advice or determine fitness to work.

Availability

The project is under development and may contain errors. Service outages, changes to Oura APIs, hardware keys, or macOS can affect behavior. No availability or enforcement guarantee is offered.

Questions

Contact the operator through the project issue tracker. Oura authorization is also subject to Oura’s own agreements.
